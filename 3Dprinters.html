3D Printers

DO NOT remove supplies from the BTU lab.

***We are updating our file management system to streamline your work. For now, please work directly with BTU staff to get your parts going.*** 3D printers in the main space must be used properly. Contact a Mischief Maker to set up experimental 3D printer usage. 3D printing workshops can be found on the BTU calender. PRUSA SLICER software is provided and required to use the 3D printers.

    Label colors:

  • Purple - 3D printing equipment and materials

    Policies

    Faculty: Class assignments requiring 3D printing should be coordinated by LAs, TAs, or instructors.

    Students: Assistance with 3D Printing for class assignments should be coordinated with class LAs/TAs first, then BTU Mischief Makers.

    Everybody: 3D printing hardware/software update frequently! Make sure you stay up to date, too.

    REQUIRED Proper usage:

  • Do not alter any part of the machine.
  • Unless agreed upon by BTU staff, PLA and ABS material should only be used.
  • Ask for assistence when using the machine before you are comfortable with it.
  • Do not use third-party slicing software; only Prusa Slicer.

Equipment

    Printers

  • Prusa Mk4 FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa Mk3S+ FDM printer (x3)
  • Prusa MINI2 FDM printer (x4)
  • FDM printers available for experimental projects; see staff!

    Filaments

  • 1.75mm PLA filament (black, and random colors)
  • 1.75mm ABS filament (black, and random colors)
