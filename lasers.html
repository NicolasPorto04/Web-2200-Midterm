1. Who Can Use

Faculty

Class assignments requiring laser cutting should be facilitated by LAs or TAs, or instructors.

Everybody

Laser cutter access is separate from general BTU access and REQUIRES AN ORIENTATION

2. Orientation

Laser cutter orientations are scheduled on the BTU Lab Calendar.

Anyone can gain access to operate the laser cutter using the process described below. Prior to that, schedule a time to complete your laser cutting job with one of the Lazor Wizards listed below, and on the BTU Mischeif Makers page.

3.access

Follow these six steps to go from your first orientation to full access.

  1. Complete the laser cutter orientation. See the calendar.
  2. Class assignments, where you need assistance, are facilitated by LAs, TAs, and instructors first, then "Lazor Wizards", if available.
  3. As long as you need assistance, schedule laser appointments with a BTU Lazor Wizard listed below and on the BTU Mischief Makers page.
  4. Lazor Wizards provide "guided access". We will help you as much, or as little as needed.
  5. When you demonstrate independent and proper use of the laser cutter, you will have full access.
  6. Block 1-hour times on the whiteboard next to each laser. (Not during scheduled orientations.)
