Lab Rules & Policies

Materials

BTU LAB IS NOT A FREE SUPPLY STORE. You can barter for what you need by displaying your work in BTU, tagging us on social media, running a workshop, or helping organize supplies. If you take something, give something back.

Rules: Be Cool

  1. Respect others, their space, and their work.
  2. Keep yourself, others, and the equipment safe.
  3. Report broken or misbehaving equipment.
  4. Never use slang or pop culture as cover to put people down for their race, age, gender, sexual orientation, ability, or any other part of who they are.
  5. Let staff know when a supply runs out.
  6. Get approval before taking any tool or material out of the lab.
  7. Protect tables and surfaces from hot glue, paint, and other messes.
  8. Keep shared work areas clear and put things back where they belong.
  9. Tell a staff member if someone is not being cool.
  10. Clean up your scraps and sweep when you are done.
  11. Get trained first, then use tools, materials, and processes the way they are meant to be used.
  12. Do not do anything that harms the BTU, ATLAS, CU, or Earth community.

Policies

  1. BTU Lab follows the policies and codes of conduct of the University of Colorado.
  2. Anyone who repeatedly ignores lab rules or policies, or disrespects others, will lose access to BTU.
