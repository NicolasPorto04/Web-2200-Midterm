Orientation

Registration is required. The woodshop orientation scheduale can be found on the BTU Lab calendar.

    Policies

    Wood shop access: This is serpeate from the BTU Lab. To acccess the wood shop, you need to complete an orientation.

    Timing: The table saw, band saw, belt sander, chop saw, drill press, and other major equipment must be fully powered off between 10pm and 6am, dailty. DO NOT tamper with timers.

    Orientation: An orientation from BTU Lab staff is required for the wood shop. Following the orientation, you must follow these conditions:

  • Do not work alone; stay within earshot of another person.
  • Do not operate a tool you are unfamiliar with; ask for assistance.
  • Do not permanantly alter any equipment.
  • Do not remove any tools from the wood shop or lab.
  • Do not attempt to repair any tools or machinery; report the issue to staff and explain what happened.
  • BTU staff may revoke any access for any reason deemed necessary.
  • Under no circumstance may wood shop policies be ignored.
  • Multiple violations of policies, by anyone, will result in reduced access for everyone.
