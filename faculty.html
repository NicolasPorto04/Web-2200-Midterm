Faculty

Welcome back! BTU Lab supports coursework, research, and experimentation for students, faculty, and staff, with up to 7,000 hours of coursework use each semester. To keep the space safe with that much demand, everyone completes short, one-time orientations before using the major equipment.

Questions? Contact Zack Weaver, Director of BTU Lab and Assistant Teaching Professor at the ATLAS Institute: zack.weaver@colorado.edu.

Getting Started

Teaching a class that will use BTU Lab? Fill out the faculty course form every semester.

The BTU Mischief Makers are our student staff. They provide instructional support on a wide range of tools and topics.
